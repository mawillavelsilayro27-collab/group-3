ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Building the Next Generation of Tech Innovators in Negros.

The BS in Computer Science program at North Negros College delivers practical experience in software development, core computer science principles, and modern technologies—starting from your first day in the lab.

Our graduates are fully prepared to excel in software engineering, system architecture, data analytics, and tech entrepreneurship.
